Top 5 Learnings from September

  1. The double-stamps promotion gave a modest order lift for a large number of points. The "Fall New Arrivals: Double Stamps" promotion ran mainly 9/24–9/26 and added 919K bonus points, 5% of the month's earn. At about 10 points = $1, that is roughly $92K of potential liability. Promo days averaged about 2,130 orders earning points, against about 2,030 on other days (+5%). The weekend of 9/19–9/20 had more orders (about 2,300–2,900 a day) with no promotion. Purchase points per day were up about 17% on promo days. Before repeating it, check whether the promotion drove incremental spend or mostly pulled purchases forward.
  2. Engagement actions fell sharply in September. Review completions were down 40% on August (948 vs. 1,591), and profile completions were down 18% (982 vs. 1,203). The points involved are small (about 48K combined), but these are the non-purchase signals of how engaged members are. They are worth watching, and possibly a prompt or small incentive.
  3. The points balance is building fast. Outstanding points rose 13.3M (+9.7%) in the month, to 151.3M. Last year's balance was roughly flat, and 15.2M points expired then. This year none expired.
  4. Redemption habits are stable, and POS is slipping slightly. The average redeemed code was about $31, in line with August. POS drove 66% of redeemed points, down from 69%, with the checkout extension and dashboard gaining share.
  5. The top of the VIP base is growing fastest. Legend grew 8.3% (10,493 members) against 3.6% for total members. That lifts the estimated Legend shipping cost to about $317K, up about $24K on August's figure.
